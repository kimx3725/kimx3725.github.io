Dongmin (Dennis) Kim

dongmin_kim [at] g [dot] harvard [dot] edu

I am an ecologist using statistical models to problems in ecology. I am also a founder/data curator of MoveDisease Archive. In my research, I am interested in using applied statistics to identify mechanisms that drive individual heterogeneity in organismal behaviors. In particular, I have been focused on how variation in animal movement is shaped by an individual's internal states such as zoonotic pathogen infection and memory.

Currently, I am a George E. Burch Postdoctoral Fellow at the Smithsonian Conservation Biology Institute. Previously, I was a postdoctoral associate in Jesús Pinto-Ledezma's lab at the University of Minnesota and Paul Moorcroft's lab at Harvard University. I received my PhD at the University of Minnesota in 2024, where I was co-advised by Allison Shaw and John Fieberg. Before my PhD, I studied statistics at the University of Minnesota, Morris.
